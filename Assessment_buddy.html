About Assessment Buddy

Assessment Buddy helps incoming undergraduates with zero-friction diagnostics plus revision tools: specialization quizzes, study guides, browser-based revision audio, sample papers, and PYQ-style practice.

Students register with their own Roll Number, Name and Stream (max 15). Results are stored per roll and visible only on the Admin dashboard (BuddyMaster).
